# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and take feedback on the work itself.
- Accept that the maintainers may decline a change that is good but out of
  scope. That is not a judgement of you or of the code.
- Remember that most people here are doing this in time they were not paid for,
  including whoever answers your issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing others' private
information, sustained disruption, or unwelcome attention. Persistently
demanding a maintainer's time is also a form of this.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space
representing the project.

Report a problem to the maintainer via
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/mohnacky/newsletter-automation/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. Reports will be handled discreetly. Consequences range
from a warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainer's discretion.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
